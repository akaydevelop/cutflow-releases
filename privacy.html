Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Cutflow is a desktop app that helps video editors plan and finish their projects. It is built so that your data stays with you: Cutflow has no accounts, no servers that store your projects, and no analytics or tracking.

What stays on your computer

Your projects, steps, revision notes, templates, stats and settings are saved in a file in Cutflow’s folder on your computer, with daily backups next to it. Video files are never copied or uploaded: Cutflow only remembers where they are on your disk.

Google Drive (optional)

If you choose Connect in Settings, Cutflow asks Google for two things:

  • Access to files Cutflow creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission). Cutflow saves one file, “Cutflow data.json”, holding the same data as the file on your computer, so you can back it up and move between your own computers. Cutflow cannot see, read or change any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address, only to show which Google account is connected.

The sign-in key Google gives Cutflow is stored on your computer only. Cutflow talks to Google directly from your computer; your data never passes through us, and we never see it. Choosing Disconnect revokes Cutflow’s access; you can also remove it any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The file stays in your Drive until you delete it.

Cutflow’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google is used only to provide the backup and sync you asked for. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not shared with anyone and not used to train AI models.

AI helper (optional)

The AI features use Ollama, a free app that runs AI models on your own computer, so the text you send to the AI never leaves it. If you change the AI server address in Settings to another server, your prompts go to that server instead, under its own terms.

Premiere Pro sync (optional)

The Premiere Pro panel talks to Cutflow over a local connection on your own computer (127.0.0.1). Nothing is sent over the internet.

Automatic updates

Cutflow checks for new versions by downloading a small file from GitHub, where its installers are published. GitHub sees the request like any web request (for example your IP address), under GitHub’s privacy statement. No information about you or your projects is sent.

Children

Cutflow is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/akaydevelop/cutflow-releases/issues.
